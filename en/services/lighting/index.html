Services

Professional lighting for events

Lighting is what separates a room full of people from an event people remember. We design the atmosphere and operate it live, matching every moment of the night.

Message us on WhatsApp Request a quote

What is included

  • Moving heads, beams and wash fixtures for the stage and dance floor
  • Architectural lighting to wash walls, columns and ceilings
  • Followspot for the guests of honour and the key moments
  • Warm, low-key light for dinner and a full change for the party
  • Live operation: lighting follows the DJ, the band and the host
  • Console, dimmers, structure and cabling included

A different design for every moment

Dinner is not lit like the dance floor. We build separate looks for arrival, dinner, the grand entrance, the toast and the dancing, and trigger them live as the night unfolds.

The technical side settled in advance

We calculate power draw, check the venue's distribution boards and decide whether a generator is needed. Lighting is the service that demands the most power, and the one that most often catches events out when it has not been planned.
