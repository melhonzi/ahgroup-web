What is included

  • Representation of Paraguayan bands and solo artists
  • Booking of other national and international acts
  • Technical production included and matched to the artist's rider
  • Transport, accommodation and tour scheduling logistics
  • Executive production so the artist only has to think about the show
  • Shows for private parties, corporate events and public concerts

Booking the show and the technical side separately is where it gets complicated

When production comes from one place and the artist from another, the rider does not match, soundcheck runs late and the show starts late. When it all comes from one place, that simply does not happen.

The backing artists look for

On stage, the artist's peace of mind is everything. We provide full support, from technical and sound through to executive production, so they can focus purely on the performance.
