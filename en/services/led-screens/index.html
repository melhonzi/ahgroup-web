LED screens for events

High-brightness LED screens for stage backdrops, totems and image reinforcement, with content operated live throughout the event.

Message us on WhatsApp Request a quote

What is included

  • Indoor and outdoor LED screens, bright enough for daytime use
  • Stage backdrop: a full wall behind the band or the host
  • Vertical LED totems for entrances, red carpet and brand areas
  • Reinforcement screens so people at the back can see clearly
  • Live camera feed, videos, photos and brand slides
  • A content operator throughout the event

Content matters as much as the screen

A huge screen with a badly exported video looks worse than a small one used well. We prepare content at the exact resolution of the modules we are rigging, and test it before the event.

Indoor and outdoor are different equipment

Outdoor screens need more brightness and weather protection; indoor screens need higher definition because people view them up close. We pick the module based on where it sits and from how far it will be seen.
