About us

15 years building events in Paraguay

AH Group Entertainment grew out of the Paraguayan nightlife scene into an event production company with its own equipment, a permanent technical crew and the ability to set up anywhere in the country.

A reputation built on delivering

The company's standing is no accident: it was built by honouring every commitment. Rigorous planning, constant communication throughout the process and a highly trained team. Every setup renews the brand.

Operational rhythm and touring reach

Keeping an active touring schedule demands precise coordination: specialised crew, high-end equipment, transport and timelines across different cities. That constant rhythm is what lets us take on productions of any size.

The backing great talent looks for

On stage, the artist's peace of mind is everything. We provide full support, from technical and sound through to executive production, so the artist can focus purely on the performance.

Solutions for the corporate sector

For companies we produce launches, anniversaries, year-end parties and conventions, with the same technical structure and the same timeline discipline as a tour.

Host and MC

Alexis Honzi, the lord of the night

Announcer, host, DJ and entertainment entrepreneur. Born in Asunción, he started in the nightlife scene around 15 years ago, first as tour manager for a music project and later hosting events. Over the years, the key figures of Paraguayan nightlife gave him the nickname "the lord of the night".

Today he is general director of AH Group and still hosts the company's events personally.
