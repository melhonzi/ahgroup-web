Municipal and government events

Official ceremonies, inaugurations, city anniversaries and municipal festivals. Production for public spaces, with the logistics and planning these events demand.

Message us on WhatsApp Request a quote

What is included

  • Stage and structure for squares, grounds and open spaces
  • Sound with coverage for large crowds
  • High-brightness LED screens for daytime use
  • Stage and public-space lighting
  • Official announcing and ceremony hosting
  • Protocol coordination and order of officials

Open space, large crowd

A square has no walls to return the sound and no power available. So we calculate coverage, cabinet distribution and our own generator before quoting, not after.

Protocol is respected

At an official ceremony the order of officials, the anthems and each speaker's time are not negotiable. It is agreed beforehand with the institution and held by the announcer throughout.
