Graduation and class parties

The party the class has been planning for years. Production for schools and universities, with logistics built for large guest counts and long nights.

Message us on WhatsApp Request a quote

What is included

  • Stage and sound sized to the real guest count
  • Dance-floor lighting built for a long party, not a dinner
  • LED screens with the class video and course photos
  • A DJ with a set built together with the graduates themselves
  • Live bands and shows booked through us
  • Effects for the class entrance and the finale

Size it right or the back row hears nothing

Graduations bring far more people than a private party, almost always in large venues. If the system is undersized, the back cannot hear and the floor splits in two. We calculate power and coverage from the real guest count, not the budgeted one.

We work with the class committee

The class has its own committee, its own budget and its own ideas. We sit down with them, build the package that fits what they raised and put in writing what is and is not included.
