What is included

  • Sound and DJ sized to the space: house, patio or venue
  • Dance-floor lighting and ambient lighting for the space
  • LED screens for videos, photos and recorded messages
  • A host or MC if you want moments properly introduced
  • Cold sparklers and low fog for the cake and the toast
  • Setup, live operation and teardown

At home, measure the power first

House parties almost always run into the same thing: the electrical installation cannot take the sound plus the lighting. We check it beforehand and bring our own generator if needed.

The neighbours count too

In residential areas it is worth agreeing beforehand on a finish time and a volume. We angle the cabinets so the party is heard inside the house and not down the whole street.
