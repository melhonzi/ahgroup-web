FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most before booking. If anything is still unclear, message us on WhatsApp.

Which areas do you cover?

We set up equipment anywhere in Paraguay, based in Asunción and Greater Asunción. For artist services we travel to any destination, inside or outside the country.

How much does producing an event cost?

We do not publish prices because every event is quoted on venue, guest count, duration and services. Tell us what you have in mind on WhatsApp and we will send a concrete quote.

Can I book a single service?

Yes. You can book sound only, LED screens only or the DJ only. That said, most clients choose the full package because it works out better and there is one person accountable for the event.

How far in advance should I book?

The earlier the better, especially for peak dates such as year-end, quinceanera season and graduation parties. Ask anyway: we often solve dates that are close.

Is the equipment your own?

Yes. We work with our own equipment and a permanent technical crew, which is what lets us respond quickly during the event without depending on third parties.

Is setup and teardown included?

Yes. Setup, technical rehearsals, operation during the event and teardown are all included in the production.

Can you book artists and bands?

Yes. We represent bands and solo acts and also arrange bookings for other artists. The advantage is that the show arrives already coordinated with the technical production.

What if it rains or the event is outdoors?

We assess it during the site visit and define equipment protection and a contingency plan before the date. We never improvise that on the day.
